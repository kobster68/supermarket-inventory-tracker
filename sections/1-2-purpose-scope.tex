\subsection{Purpose and Scope of the SAD}
\label{sec:purpose-scope}

This document specifies the software architecture of the Regional Supermarket
Inventory Tracking System, which tracks inventory across a chain of supermarket
stores and a central distribution center and drives the shipping orders that move
product from the distribution center to the stores. It is the single place where
the architecture is recorded. Following the SEI Views and Beyond template, we
document the architecture as a set of views together with the information that
applies across views.

We treat a design decision as architectural, and therefore record it here, when it
concerns the elements of the system, their externally visible properties, and the
relationships among them; we leave a decision to downstream design when it concerns
only the private internals of a single element (Bass, Clements, and Kazman 2).

This first draft is a thin vertical slice. It carries one key view in full --- a
Components-and-Connectors view of the runtime system --- and a full interface
specification for the key component, the \CFE. The remaining required views, a
Module view and an Allocation view, and the sections that depend on them, are named
and scoped here but deferred to the second draft. Every section that is not yet
written says so explicitly rather than being omitted.
