\subsection{Purpose and Scope of the SAD}
\label{sec:purpose-scope}

This document specifies the software architecture of the Regional Supermarket
Inventory Tracking System, which tracks inventory across a chain of supermarket
stores and a central distribution center and drives the shipping orders that move
product from the distribution center to the stores. It is the single place where
the architecture is recorded. Following the SEI Views and Beyond template, we
document the architecture as a set of views together with the information that
applies across views. The draft utility tree (Table~\ref{tab:utility-tree}) rates
every scenario and marks the two (H,~H) scenarios whose delivery path this draft
specifies.

We treat a design decision as architectural, and therefore record it here, when it
concerns the elements of the system, their externally visible properties, and the
relationships among them; we leave a decision to downstream design when it concerns
only the private internals of a single element (Bass, Clements, and Kazman 2).

This first draft is a thin vertical slice. It carries two views. The module
decomposition view is the primary view: the functional decomposition of the system,
close to a domain model, from which the later module views grow. The
Components-and-Connectors view is the key runtime view, driven by the scenario the
team rates highest in value and risk --- the guaranteed delivery of store events to
the distribution center. The draft also carries a full interface specification for
the key component, the \CFE. The remaining required view, an Allocation view, and the
sections that depend on it, are named and scoped here but deferred to the second
draft. Every section that is not yet written says so explicitly rather than being
omitted.
