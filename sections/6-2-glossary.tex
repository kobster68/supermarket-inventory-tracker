\subsection{Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begingroup
\small
\clubpenalty=10000
\widowpenalty=10000
\begin{description}[style=unboxed,leftmargin=1em,labelindent=0pt,itemsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]
\item[Store Agent] The store component that applies sales and stock changes locally, queues their events in the Outbox, and raises shipping orders at minimum stock for store-manager confirmation; recovery from durable local state must restore inventory access within the 30-minute outage limit.
\item[Store-Local Inventory] The store's own inventory copy, updated locally first so access can continue during communication outages, subject to surviving local services and storage capacity.
\item[Outbox] The durable, ordered queue holding committed inventory events and confirmed shipping orders until central acceptance is saved locally; pruning its entries preserves separate local recovery history.
\item[Communications Front-End (CFE)] The distribution-center Publish-Subscribe event bus that persists messages before publisher acceptance, distributes authorized source streams and retains replay history according to cumulative subscriber progress.
\item[Inventory Exchange Protocol (IEP)] The interface for publication, acceptance and processing acknowledgements, source subscriptions, replay and resync; its messages include Inventory Event, Shipping Order, Acknowledgement and Subscription.
\item[Inventory Event] One change to a store's or the distribution center's stock --- event ID, source, product, quantity delta, timestamp, and per-source sequence number --- emitted at up to 100 per second per store.
\item[Shipping Order] A replenishment request raised by a store at minimum stock and confirmed by its manager before publication through the Outbox to the Communications Front-End and Shipping-Order Fulfillment.
\item[Acknowledgement] Either publisher acceptance from the Communications Front-End after durable persistence, or subscriber confirmation after durable processing or local ignoring; each carries cumulative per-source progress.
\item[Cumulative acknowledgement] Confirmation of the highest contiguous source sequence accepted or processed, including every earlier position; it can confirm several messages together.
\item[Central Master Inventory] The authoritative projection of store and warehouse stock from inventory events; each update commits together with duplicate-detection state and its processing cursor.
\item[DC Agent] The distribution-center component that publishes warehouse inventory changes, including shipment dispatches, through the Communications Front-End for the Central Master Inventory to apply.
\item[Subscriber] A consumer that filters authorized source streams and acknowledges durable handling progress; the named consumers are the Central Master Inventory, Shipping-Order Fulfillment, Store-Tracking Client, Reporting Client and future External Analytics Client.
\item[Shipping-Order Fulfillment] The distribution-center subscriber that receives shipping orders raised by the stores, reads the master record, and presents each order to the DC manager for fulfilment.
\item[Store-Tracking Client] A subscriber that follows inventory events to track stock at the store level.
\item[Reporting Client] A subscriber that answers manager queries such as lowest stock, overstocked items, and the total value of a product family.
\item[External Analytics Client] A future external, read-only subscriber that receives inventory events over the Inventory Exchange Protocol; it cannot modify stock or orders.
\item[POS Adapter] The store-side boundary component that turns the external point-of-sale system's sales records into inventory events.
\item[Store] One supermarket in the regional chain; it holds its own inventory locally, with a 99\% availability requirement for inventory access.
\item[Distribution Center (DC)] The central hub that ships product to the stores and hosts the Communications Front-End and the Central Master Inventory.
\item[External system] A system outside our boundary reached through a defined interface: the point-of-sale system at each store or the future External Analytics Client.
\item[Product family] A grouping of products and nested families at arbitrary depth; a product may belong to more than one family.
\item[Composite] The GoF structural pattern that gives products and product families a common catalog interface, with recursive operations over nested families; our model must additionally prevent cycles and repeated counting (Gamma et al. 163).
\item[Scenario ID] The frozen identifier of a quality-attribute scenario (for example, \texttt{AV-1}) that the interface specification and the tactics table reference.
\item[Key view] The Components-and-Connectors view of runtime interactions; its key component is the Communications Front-End.
\item[Local transaction] A group of changes committed together within one local database, such as an inventory update and its outgoing event, with atomicity, consistency, isolation and durability guarantees.
\item[At-least-once delivery] A delivery model that retries to achieve at least one delivery under the stated failure assumptions and may deliver duplicates; retries retain the original event identifier.
\item[Idempotent processing] Applying an event's business effect once despite repeated delivery, using its stable identifier and atomically recording successful processing with the associated state change.
\item[Per-source sequence] A persistent consecutive number shared by inventory events and shipping orders from one source; it establishes source order and supports gap detection and replay.
\item[Retention window] A further 60 minutes after all relevant active subscriptions acknowledge through a message, or after acceptance when none is relevant. Unread messages remain retained while their subscriptions are disconnected.
\item[Snapshot] A saved representation of state at a known point; recovery may also require subsequent committed history not contained in that snapshot.
\item[Recovery time] The interval from failure occurrence to restored usable service, including detection, restart and state recovery.
\item[Eventual consistency] The property that local and central inventory may temporarily differ but converge when retained events are delivered and processed under the stated recovery assumptions.
\item[Module] An implementation unit of the system's code structure; the element type of the module decomposition view, related to other modules by is-part-of (decomposition) and uses (dependency).
\item[Module decomposition view] The primary view of code modules and their relationships.
\end{description}
\endgroup
