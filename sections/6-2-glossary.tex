\subsection{Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=1em,labelindent=0pt]
\item[Store Agent] The runtime component in each store that receives inventory events from the point-of-sale system (through the POS Adapter) and local stock operations, updates the Store-Local Inventory, and forwards events through the Outbox; recovery from durable local state must restore inventory access within the 30-minute outage limit.
\item[Store-Local Inventory] The store's own inventory copy, updated locally first so access can continue during communication outages, subject to surviving local services and storage capacity.
\item[Outbox] The durable, ordered queue associated with the Store Agent that retains inventory events until the Communications Front-End acknowledges durable acceptance; delivery depends on surviving storage, available capacity and eventual reconnection.
\item[Communications Front-End (CFE)] The publish--subscribe broker at the distribution center that persists each inventory event, acknowledges it, and publishes it by topic to every subscriber; it is the key component of this draft, and its interface is the Inventory Exchange Protocol.
\item[Inventory Exchange Protocol (IEP)] The provided-and-required interface of the Communications Front-End: publish, acknowledge, subscribe and unsubscribe by topic, replay-from-sequence, and resync, over the message types Inventory Event, Acknowledgement, and Subscription.
\item[Inventory Event] One change to a store's or the distribution center's stock --- event ID, source, product, quantity delta, timestamp, and per-source sequence number --- emitted at up to 100 per second per store.
\item[Acknowledgement] The confirmation the Communications Front-End returns once it has durably persisted an inventory event.
\item[Central Master Inventory] The authoritative record of all store and distribution-center inventory, built by subscribing to every inventory event from every Store Agent and the DC Agent.
\item[DC Agent] The runtime component at the distribution center that publishes the center's own inventory events (goods receipts and shipment dispatches) through the Communications Front-End, so the master record also covers distribution-center stock.
\item[Subscriber] Any component that subscribes to the Communications Front-End; the named subscribers are the Central Master Inventory, Shipping-Order Generation, the Store-Tracking Client, and the Reporting Client, and their number is variable by design.
\item[Shipping-Order Generation] The distribution-center subscriber that computes shipping orders from minimum stock quantities and real-time sales and sends purchase and shipping orders to the supplier system through the Supplier Gateway.
\item[Store-Tracking Client] A subscriber that follows inventory events to track stock at the store level.
\item[Reporting Client] A subscriber that answers manager queries such as lowest stock, overstocked items, and the total value of a product family.
\item[POS Adapter] The boundary element inside the Store Agent that turns the external point-of-sale system's sales records into inventory events.
\item[Supplier Gateway] The boundary element at the distribution center that exchanges orders and goods-receipt confirmations with the external supplier/ERP system.
\item[Store] One supermarket in the regional chain; it holds a local copy of its own inventory and must be able to read it 99\% of the time.
\item[Distribution Center (DC)] The central hub that ships product to the stores and hosts the Communications Front-End and the Central Master Inventory.
\item[External system] A system outside our boundary reached through a defined interface --- the point-of-sale system at each store and the supplier/ERP system at the distribution center.
\item[Product family] A grouping of products that can be nested to an arbitrary depth.
\item[Scenario ID] The frozen identifier of a quality-attribute scenario (for example, \texttt{AV-1}) that the interface specification and the tactics table reference.
\item[Key view] The Components-and-Connectors view of the runtime system delivered in this first draft; the key component it specifies is the Communications Front-End.
\item[Local transaction] A group of changes committed together within one local database, such as an inventory update and its outgoing event, with atomicity, consistency, isolation and durability guarantees.
\item[At-least-once delivery] A delivery model that retries to achieve at least one delivery under the stated failure assumptions and may deliver duplicates; retries retain the original event identifier.
\item[Idempotent processing] Applying an event's business effect once despite repeated delivery, using its stable identifier and atomically recording successful processing with the associated state change.
\item[Per-source sequence] The persistent sequence number assigned within one event source; it establishes that source's ordering and supports gap detection and replay.
\item[Snapshot] A saved representation of state at a known point; recovery may also require subsequent committed history not contained in that snapshot.
\item[Recovery time] The interval from failure occurrence to restored usable service, including detection, restart and state recovery.
\item[Eventual consistency] The property that local and central inventory may temporarily differ but converge when retained events are delivered and processed under the stated recovery assumptions.
\item[Module] An implementation unit of the system's code structure; the element type of the module decomposition view, related to other modules by is-part-of (decomposition) and uses (dependency).
\item[Module decomposition view] The primary view of this draft: the functional decomposition of the system into modules --- the Store Subsystem, the Communications Front-End, the Distribution Center Subsystem, and a Common module of shared domain types and the Inventory Exchange Protocol library --- from which the runtime components of the key view grow.
\end{description}
