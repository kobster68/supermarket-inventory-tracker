\subsubsection{Requirements Coverage}
\label{sec:requirements-coverage}
We trace the brief's requirements to scenarios, tactics and responsible elements below.
These are design mappings, not evidence that the requirements have been demonstrated.
Tactic definitions, textbook pages and decision status appear in
Section~\ref{sec:approaches}; (P) marks a proposed refinement. Pattern names below
refer to Publish-Subscribe (Bass, Clements, and Kazman 129--130) and Composite
(Gamma et al. 163).
