\subsubsection{Summary of Background Changes Reflected in Current Version}
\label{sec:background-changes}
The first-draft review removed supplier/ERP integration and portability from scope.
We assume sufficient \DC stock. Orders now originate in the store, are confirmed by
its manager and reach \ShippingOrderFulfillment at the \DC. The future
\AnalyticsClient replaces the former external integration in the system context.
Source streams and local filtering replace family topics; cumulative subscriber
acknowledgements govern replay retention. This draft also addresses the (H,M)
scenarios and adds the \scnref{PERF-4} catch-up analysis.
Section~\ref{sec:doc-control} records integrated draft milestones.
