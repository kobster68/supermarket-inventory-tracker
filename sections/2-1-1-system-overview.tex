\subsubsection{System Overview}
\label{sec:system-overview}

The system tracks the inventory of a regional supermarket chain. The chain has three
stores and one distribution center (\DC) today, and it will grow to ten stores. The
\DC ships product to the stores.

Each store records its sales in real time. The point-of-sale (POS) system in the store
sends each sale to the system, which turns it into an \InventoryEvent. A store emits up
to 100 events per second. The store keeps its own copy of its inventory, the
\StoreLocalInventory, so that store managers can read and change it when the network to
the \DC is down.

The \DC keeps the \CentralMasterInventory, the master record of the stock in every
store and in the \DC. The store events reach the \DC through the \CFE, which also sends
them to a variable number of other clients. From minimum stock quantities and current
sales, the \DC makes shipping orders. It sends purchase and shipping orders to the
external supplier/ERP system.

Products belong to product families, and a family can contain other families to any
depth. Managers ask for three types of report: the lowest-stock items, the overstocked
items, and the total value of a product family.
