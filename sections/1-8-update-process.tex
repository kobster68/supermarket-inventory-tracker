\subsection{Process for Updating this SAD}
\label{sec:update-process}
We maintain LaTeX and PlantUML sources in GitHub, following the SEI Views and Beyond
structure. Changes use branch-based pull requests with another author's review of the
source and PDF before merge. CI compiles the PDF and runs lint checks. The revision
history (Section~\ref{sec:doc-control}) records integrated draft and submission milestones.

We coordinate through Slack and divide work into four slices: Leon Letournel owns
overview, context and requirements, reviewed by Mike Andrews; Godson Umoren owns runtime
and module views, reviewed by Kobe Laprade; Kobe Laprade owns the \IEP specification,
reviewed by Leon Letournel; Mike Andrews owns approaches, tactics and analysis, reviewed
by Godson Umoren. Later drafts extend these areas through the same review process.
