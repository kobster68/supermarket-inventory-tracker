\subsection{How a View Is Documented}
\label{sec:how-view-documented}

Each view in Section~\ref{sec:views} is documented with the view-packet structure of
the SEI Views and Beyond template. A view packet has a \emph{primary presentation},
usually a diagram, that shows the view's elements and the relations among them; an
\emph{element catalog} that details the elements, their relations, their interfaces,
their behavior, and any constraints; a \emph{context diagram} that fixes the boundary
of the part of the system shown; a \emph{variability guide} that records where and
when the architecture can be varied; and \emph{architecture background} that gives
the rationale for decisions whose scope is limited to the view. Every view is
presented as a diagram supported by narrative, and every diagram carries a key and a
caption that names the view type.
