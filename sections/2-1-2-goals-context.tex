\subsubsection{Goals and Context}
\label{sec:goals-context}

The system has four goals:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item keep the store inventory available to the store, even when the link to the
  \DC fails;
  \item keep one accurate master record of all stock, with no lost store event;
  \item create shipping orders at the store, and give reports, that managers understand
  with minimal training;
  \item let the chain add stores and clients without a change to the existing parts.
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:context} shows the system boundary. Inside it are the stores, the \DC and
the WAN link between them. Outside it are the store and \DC managers and the two external
systems: the POS system in each store and the \AnalyticsClient, a future subscriber.
Table~\ref{tab:external-exchange} lists the information that we exchange with each
external system.
