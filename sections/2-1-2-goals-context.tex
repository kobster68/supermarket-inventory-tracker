\subsubsection{Goals and Context}
\label{sec:goals-context}

The system has four goals:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item keep the store inventory available to the store, even when the network to the
  \DC fails;
  \item keep one accurate master record of all stock, with no lost store event;
  \item make shipping orders and reports that managers understand with minimal
  training;
  \item let the chain add stores and clients without a change to the existing parts.
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:context} shows the system boundary. Inside it are the stores and the
\DC. Outside it are the two external systems, the POS system in each store and the
supplier/ERP system, and the store and \DC managers. Table~\ref{tab:external-exchange}
lists the information that we exchange with each external system.
